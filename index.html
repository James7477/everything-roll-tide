🐘 Why an elephant named Big Al?

The team is called the Crimson Tide, but its famous elephant represents a story about the power of Alabama football.

1930 · The Red Elephants

During Alabama's game against Ole Miss, the enormous varsity squad impressed the crowd. A fan reportedly called out, “Hold your horses, the elephants are coming!” Sportswriter Everett Strupper retold the scene, and writers began calling the crimson-clad players the Red Elephants.

1979 · Why the name Big Al?

Students chose Big Al in a campus vote. The University of Alabama's student traditions guide says the name also honored well-known campus DJ Al Brown. The official costumed Big Al made his debut at the 1979 Sugar Bowl, long after the football team was nicknamed the Red Elephants.

The Band & Cheerleaders

Yes! Big Al is Alabama's university mascot, not a separate mascot for each group. The Million Dollar Band and cheerleaders share that spirit. They're part of the Elephant Stomp, a pregame gathering at Gorgas Library where the band performs and cheerleaders lead fans.

📣 Alabama Cheerleading

Spirit Squad Heritage

Two Teams. One Crimson Tide.

Alabama Athletics recognizes Coed and All Girl cheer squads. The Coed team supports home and away football games; All Girl supports home football games and other Alabama sports events.

1940 · Coed program tradition

The Alabama Cheer Alumni Association identifies 1940 as the Coed program's founding year.

2011 · All Girl program

The same alumni association marks 2011 as the establishment of the All Girl program.

1980 · Big Al tradition

The alumni association dates the Big Al program to 1980.

🥁 The Million Dollar Band

The Sound of Bryant-Denny

From 14 Musicians to 400+

1912 · The beginning

The marching band started with 14 musicians under Dr. Gustav Wittig, according to Alabama's band history.

1922 · A million-dollar name

After a Georgia Tech game, alumnus W. C. “Champ” Pickens is credited with the phrase “Million Dollar Band.” Stories differ on the exact exchange, but the nickname stuck.

2003 · National recognition

The Million Dollar Band received the Sudler Trophy, a major college marching band honor.

What Makes a Saturday Sound Like Alabama?

Members include instrumental musicians, the Crimsonettes and the Color Guard. Fans know the pregame energy, fight-song traditions, and the pregame Elephant Stomp on the Gorgas Library steps when scheduled.

Fun fact: The 1922 band had to raise travel money from merchants to get to Georgia Tech. That old road-trip struggle helped give the band its legendary name!

Travel note: Not every road game carries the whole band. Pep-band participation and travel arrangements depend on the event, host allotment and arrangements.

🚌 What It Takes to Take the Tide on the Road

Behind the scenes

Think of a road game as moving a traveling football operation: players, coaches, trainers, equipment, food, buses, lodging, security, filming and communications. These specifics come from reporting on Alabama—not a claim to know the team's private 2026 itinerary.

01 · DAYS BEFORE

Plans, Rooms & Seats

Travel staff coordinate hotel room blocks, coaches' meeting rooms, passenger manifests, local transportation, meals and any air charter. Alabama partnered with Anthony Travel in 2024 for team travel management.

02 · THURSDAY

Load the Equipment

In a detailed 2019 account, Alabama equipment staff began packing Thursday. Their road transport used an 18-wheeler carrying helmets, pads, uniforms, sideline supplies and other gear.

03 · FRIDAY

Travel & Hotel

The team and staff travel to the destination by buses and/or aircraft, depending on the trip. An older Alabama road-trip report documented four team buses plus a bus for managers and athletic trainers for a Mississippi State trip.

04 · NIGHT BEFORE

Meetings & Fuel

Position meetings, film, walk-throughs, athletic training, meals and rest are all coordinated. A team nutrition plan covers dinners, breakfasts, pregame meals and snacks.

05 · GAME DAY

Make the Stadium Ready

Equipment staff prepare a visiting locker room, sort dozens of uniforms, distribute helmets and pads, and stock the sideline before warmups. Medical and support personnel are on hand.

06 · AFTER THE WHISTLE

Pack and Get Home

Uniforms, equipment, communications and medical supplies are packed and inventoried for the return. Travel crews may work long after fans have left the stadium.

📦 The Equipment Truck Is a Big Deal

A 2019 account describes how the Alabama road truck departs after Thursday practice and how equipment staff arrive at the stadium roughly four hours before kickoff. Their responsibilities include setting up and eventually packing the locker room, sometimes hours after the game.

And here is an important twist: Alabama's historical equipment operation even used a 26-foot box truck for home games, moving equipment from practice facilities to Bryant-Denny.
